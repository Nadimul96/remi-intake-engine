INVESTMENT COMMITTEE MEMORANDUM

TO: Investment Committee
FROM: Alex Donovan, Senior Associate
DATE: November 10, 2020
RE: Acquisition of Oakwood Commons — APPROVED

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Synthetic Capital Partners proposes the acquisition of Oakwood Commons, a 232-unit multifamily community in Houston's Westchase submarket. The asset fits Fund II's value-add strategy with significant renovation upside and strong demographic tailwinds.

INVESTMENT THESIS
• Value-add opportunity on 45+ units with $8K/unit renovation program
• In-place rents 8% below market with clear path to $175/month premiums
• 2008 vintage with 2018 renovation — newer construction with limited deferred maintenance
• Westchase submarket showing 2.5% population growth and 3.2% job growth

ACQUISITION SUMMARY
Purchase Price: $41,200,000 ($177,586/unit)
Closing Costs: $825,000
Total Cost: $42,025,000

FINANCING
Senior Loan: $13,800,000 (75% LTV)
Interest Rate: 4.25% fixed
Term: 5-year interest only
DSCR (stabilized): 1.31x

PERFORMANCE METRICS
Entry Cap Rate: 5.6%
Levered IRR: 17.2%
Equity Multiple: 2.0x
5-Year Hold, Exit at 5.5% cap

SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS
Scenario          Entry Cap    Exit Cap    IRR
Base Case         5.6%         5.5%        17.2%
Upside            5.6%         5.0%        19.8%
Downside          6.0%         6.0%        14.1%

RISK FACTORS
• Houston market concentration (mitigated: diverse employer base)
• Value-add execution risk (mitigated: Greenfield PM experience)
• Interest rate risk (mitigated: 5-year fixed rate)

RECOMMENDATION
Approve acquisition at $41.2M with First Western financing terms.
