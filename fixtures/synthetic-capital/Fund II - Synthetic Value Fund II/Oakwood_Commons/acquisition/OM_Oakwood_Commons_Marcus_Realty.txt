2. INVESTMENT HIGHLIGHTS

ATTRACTIVE SUBMARKET DYNAMICS
The Westchase submarket is one of Houston's most desirable infill locations, benefiting from proximity to the Energy Corridor, major employment centers, and extensive retail amenities. The submarket has demonstrated consistent rent growth averaging 3.2% annually over the past five years.

STRONG DEMOGRAPHIC PROFILE
The Property's location provides immediate access to Houston's affluent Energy Corridor workforce. The average household income within a 3-mile radius exceeds $85,000, with a median age of 34 years. The area has experienced 8.3% population growth since 2015.

RECENT CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS
The current ownership completed significant exterior renovations in 2018, including:
- New roof on all buildings ($380,000)
- Exterior paint and siding repair ($240,000)
- Pool and amenity area renovation ($180,000)
- Landscaping upgrades ($120,000)
- Parking lot resurfacing ($95,000)
Total invested: $1,015,000

These improvements position the Property competitively and reduce near-term capital expenditure requirements.

IN-PLACE RENT BELOW MARKET
Current average in-place rents are approximately 8-12% below market rates for comparable renovated units. Recent lease transactions at the Property demonstrate achievable premiums, with renovated 2BR units leasing at $1,420-1,480 compared to $1,250-1,300 for original units.

OPERATIONAL UPSIDE
Current ownership self-manages the Property with an internal property management team. A professional third-party management company could potentially reduce operating expenses by 5-8% through economies of scale and more efficient vendor relationships.

FAVORABLE FINANCING ENVIRONMENT
Current market conditions support attractive financing options. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac multifamily loans are available at sub-4.0% rates for qualified borrowers, with 75% LTV leverage readily achievable.

4. LOCATION ANALYSIS

MACRO LOCATION
Houston, Texas is the fourth-largest city in the United States and the largest city in the South. The Houston metropolitan area has a population of approximately 7.1 million and has consistently ranked among the fastest-growing major metros in the country.

SUBMARKET: WESTCHASE
The Westchase submarket is located in west Houston, approximately 12 miles from downtown and 8 miles from the Energy Corridor. The submarket is characterized by:

- High household incomes ($85,000+ average)
- Young professional demographic (median age 34)
- Strong employment base (Energy Corridor, Westchase District)
- Extensive retail and dining options
- Excellent school districts (Alief ISD, Spring Branch ISD)

PROXIMITY TO MAJOR EMPLOYMENT

Energy Corridor: 8 miles (15-minute commute)
- Major employers: BP, Shell, ConocoPhillips, Citgo
- 80,000+ employees

Westchase District: 2 miles (5-minute commute)
- Major employers: Halliburton, ABB, BMC Software
- 15,000+ employees

Texas Medical Center: 15 miles (25-minute commute)
- World's largest medical complex
- 106,000+ employees

Galleria/Uptown: 10 miles (20-minute commute)
- Houston's premier retail and office district
- 25,000+ employees

RETAIL AND AMENITIES

Westchase Shopping Center: 0.5 miles
- HEB grocery store
- Target
- Multiple restaurants and services

Westheimer Road Corridor: Adjacent
- Extensive dining and retail options

WESTCHASE DISTRICT AMENITIES
