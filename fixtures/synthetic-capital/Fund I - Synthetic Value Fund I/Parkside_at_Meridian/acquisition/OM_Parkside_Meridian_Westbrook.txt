INVESTMENT HIGHLIGHTS

STABILIZED CASH FLOW
The Property has achieved 94.6% average occupancy over the trailing 12 months with consistent rental collections. Current in-place rents are at market rates with minimal lease-up risk.

ATTRACTIVE SUBMARKET LOCATION
The Medical Center submarket benefits from proximity to the South Texas Medical Center, one of the largest medical complexes in the United States with over 35,000 employees. The area has experienced 12% job growth over the past 5 years.

RECENT RENOVATIONS
The 2014 renovation program updated unit interiors in 40% of the property, achieving rent premiums of $75-100 per month. The remaining units present an opportunity to complete the renovation program.

BELOW-REPLACEMENT COST
At $68,919 per unit, the asking price represents a significant discount to replacement cost estimated at $110,000+ per unit for comparable new construction.

FAVORABLE FINANCING ENVIRONMENT
Current market supports 75-80% LTV financing at sub-4.5% interest rates, enabling attractive leveraged returns.
